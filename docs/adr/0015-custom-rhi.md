# The Engine has its own thin graphics layer over Vulkan, Metal, and D3D12

The Engine talks to the GPU through its own small abstraction, not through wgpu or another portability layer. The renderer depends on 64-bit atomic max for the visibility buffer (ADR 0005), indirect and compute-driven dispatch, bindless resources, and virtual texture residency control, and a portability layer exposes only the intersection of its backends and tends to lag on these. Owning the layer also lets it match the Slang output (ADR 0008) directly. We accept the cost of writing and maintaining three backends and their synchronization; the layer is kept thin by exposing only what the renderer needs, not a general-purpose graphics API. Vulkan and Metal come first, with D3D12 after, because D3D12 support on the floor GPU is still unverified (ADR 0005).
