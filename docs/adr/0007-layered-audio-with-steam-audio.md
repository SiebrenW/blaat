# Audio is a layered open-source stack: a mixer graph plus Steam Audio for spatialization

The Engine owns audio integration. A mixer graph handles device output, streaming, music, and SFX, and Steam Audio provides HRTF, occlusion, propagation, and reverb, driven by Interior and Portal data. Reverb is configurable per Interior, with baked reverb data from the Bake at the high end and a cheap parametric reverb as the floor path for weak GPUs and CPUs (ADR 0006). We rejected FMOD and Wwise because their licenses treat engines as non-game products needing custom terms, and we rejected a single lightweight library because immersion and Interior-aware propagation would have to be rebuilt by hand. Steam Audio's Embree ray tracer is x86-only, so Apple silicon uses the default ray tracer and bakes more slowly. The mixer (Firewheel is the current candidate, pre-1.0 with frequent breaking releases) sits behind an Engine-owned interface so it can be replaced.
